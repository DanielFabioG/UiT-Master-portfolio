Introduction

The relationship between CO2 and GDP

We are going to explore the relationship between CO2 emissions and GDP using time series data. The analysis will be conducted using the Johansen cointegration test and Vector Error Correction Model (VECM) to understand both the long-run and short-run dynamics between these variables.

Developing countries

Further we are choosing to look at developing countries, to see if the relationship between CO2 emissions and GDP differs from that of developed countries. This is important as developing countries may have different economic structures and environmental policies that could influence this relationship.

China and USA

The two countries we will focus on are for non-OECD: China and the for OECD country we are choosing USA. China is the largest emitter of CO2 and has experienced rapid economic growth, while the USA has a long history of industrialization and is also a major emitter.
